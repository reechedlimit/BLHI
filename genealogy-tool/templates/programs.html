Our Programs

Three interconnected pillars — each one a bridge between past and future, loss and reclamation, silence and story.

🌳
Pillar One

Genealogy Documentation Tool

Our flagship digital platform purpose-built for Black family history research. Unlike generic genealogy tools, our platform is designed with the unique challenges of African American lineage in mind — navigating the 1870 Census Wall, Freedmen's Bureau records, and oral history preservation.

  • Multi-generational family tree builder with smart record suggestions
  • Secure digital vault for census records, certificates, and photographs
  • Freedmen's Bureau record integration and indexing
  • Oral history recording and transcription tools
  • Collaborative family research with shared access

Pillar Two

Community Workshops

Hands-on, in-person and virtual workshops that teach the art and science of Black genealogy research. From beginner "Where Do I Start?" sessions to advanced archival research techniques, we meet every family at their point of need.

  • Beginner genealogy workshops — start your research journey with expert guidance
  • Advanced document analysis — learn to read census records, plantation ledgers, and more
  • DNA and genetic genealogy — understand how DNA testing connects to your research
  • Oral history preservation — capture family stories before they're lost
  • Heritage tours — visit historical sites and archives with expert guides

Pillar Three

Archival Preservation

We preserve what others let crumble. Our archival program digitizes at-risk records, indexes historically significant documents, and creates a permanent digital home for Black family history materials that might otherwise be lost forever.

  • Digitization of family Bibles, letters, photographs, and certificates
  • Freedmen's Bureau record indexing and transcription
  • Census record extraction and family linking
  • Community archive partnerships with local historical societies
  • Digital preservation training for community organizations
