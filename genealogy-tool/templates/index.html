Reclaiming What Was Taken

For generations, Black family histories were systematically fragmented. The 1870 "Census Wall" stands as a barrier for many, but it is not the end of the story.

The Black Lineage & Heritage Institute gives you the tools to piece together your heritage and pass it down with pride.

"We are not defined by what was stolen from us. We are defined by what we rebuild."
