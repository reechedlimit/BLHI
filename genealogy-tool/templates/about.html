About the Project

The Family History Documentation Tool is the digital heartbeat of the Black Lineage & Heritage Institute (BLHI). We believe that every name matters, every story counts, and every lineage lives.

Our Mission

We provide the tools and archives necessary for Black individuals to reclaim their erased histories. By documenting our roots, we turn the pain of lost identity into the power of rediscovered belonging.

The Tools of Reclamation

  • Document Lineages — Build family trees that honor every ancestor.
  • Digital Archive — Preserve census records, certificates, and precious family photos.
  • Oral Histories — Record the stories and memories that form our collective heritage.
  • Data Portability — Your history belongs to you. Export your data at any time.

Our Vision

A world where the 1870 "Census Wall" is no longer a barrier, but a starting point for rediscovery. We envision a future where every Black family has a permanent, dignified record of their heritage.
