# Event Plan: Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

## 1. Virtual Soft Launch (Beta Users)
**Objective:** Test the genealogy tool with a friendly audience, gather feedback, and build early momentum.
**Target:** 50-100 invited beta users (family historians, students, community leaders).
**Format:** 60-minute Zoom/Webinar.

### Agenda
- **00:00-00:10:** Welcome & The Mission (Founders).
- **00:10-00:25:** The Problem of Erasure: Why the Institute exists now.
- **00:25-00:45:** Live Demo: Documentation Tool & Digital Archive.
- **00:45-00:55:** Feedback Session & Q&A.
- **00:55-01:00:** Next Steps: How to sign up and start documenting.

### Success Metrics
- 80% attendance rate.
- 50% of attendees log their first record within 48 hours.
- Qualitative feedback on tool usability.

---

## 2. "Family History Discovery Workshop" Series
**Objective:** Provide educational value to the community while promoting Institute tools.
**Format:** Monthly 90-minute virtual workshops.

### Proposed Topics
1. **The First Steps:** How to interview elders and organize existing family papers.
2. **Navigating the Gap:** Finding ancestors in the 1870 Census and beyond.
3. **Beyond Names & Dates:** How to write compelling family narratives from dry records.
4. **Digital Preservation:** Scanning, storing, and sharing your family archive.

### Format
- **Expert Lecture (30m):** Led by a guest genealogist or historian.
- **Hands-on Lab (45m):** Breakout rooms where participants work on their own trees using the BLHI tool.
- **Group Sharing (15m):** Discoveries and "brick walls" discussion.
