# Preliminary Grant Research: Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

## Federal Opportunities

### 1. National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH)
**Program:** [Public Humanities Projects](https://www.neh.gov/grants/public/public-humanities-projects)
- **Objective:** Supports projects that bring the ideas and insights of the humanities to life for general audiences through public programming.
- **Alignment:** Perfect for our public workshops, digital archive exhibitions, and community discovery series.
- **Deadline:** December 9, 2026 (Upcoming).
- **Amount:** $50,000 to $400,000.
- **Status:** Requires 501(c)(3) status or a fiscal sponsor.

**Program:** [Collections Stewardship](https://www.neh.gov/grants/preservation/collections-stewardship)
- **Objective:** Supports projects that address the preservation and access of humanities collections.
- **Alignment:** Ideal for our work in digitizing and archiving family records that are currently at risk.
- **Deadline:** December 15, 2026.
- **Amount:** Varies.

**Program:** [Digital Humanities Advancement Grants](https://www.neh.gov/grants/odh/digital-humanities-advancement-grants)
- **Objective:** Supports innovative, experimental, or computationally challenging digital projects at different stages of their lifecycles.
- **Alignment:** Directly supports the ongoing development of our genealogy documentation tool and database.
- **Cycle:** Usually has multiple deadlines per year.

## Private Foundation Opportunities

### 2. Andrew W. Mellon Foundation
**Strategy:** [Public Knowledge](https://www.mellon.org/strategy/public-knowledge)
- **Objective:** To support the creation, preservation, and dissemination of the knowledge that is essential to a diverse and democratic society.
- **Alignment:** This is the most direct alignment for a heritage-focused tech nonprofit. They focus on "the power of memory" and "archival preservation."
- **Inquiry:** Often requires a Letter of Inquiry (LOI) first.

### 3. Ford Foundation
**Program:** [Creativity and Free Expression](https://www.fordfoundation.org/work/our-grants/creativity-and-free-expression/)
- **Objective:** Supporting cultural institutions that give voice to the marginalized and foster social justice.
- **Alignment:** Our mission to "reclaim lost stories" and "rediscover identity" as a form of social power aligns with their focus on narrative change.
- **Cycle:** Rolling applications/inquiries.

### 4. Doris Duke Foundation
**Program:** [Arts](https://www.dorisduke.org/funding-areas/arts/)
- **Objective:** While often focused on performing arts, they have historically supported cultural preservation and the "heritage" sector within Black communities.
