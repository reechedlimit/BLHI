# Celebrity & Ambassador Outreach: Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

## Strategy Overview
We approach these high-profile individuals as partners in a historic mission. The tone is respectful, acknowledging their existing contributions to Black culture, and inviting them to leave a legacy through the Institute.

---

## 1. Dr. Henry Louis Gates Jr. (Priority 1)
**Angle:** Archival Partnership & Expert Endorsement
**Subject:** Invitation to Partner: The Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

Dear Dr. Gates,

Your work with *Finding Your Roots* has sparked a global movement of reclamation, showing millions the profound power of knowing where they come from. At the Black Lineage & Heritage Institute, we are building a permanent digital home for that movement—a nonprofit dedicated to providing every Black family with the tools to document and preserve their specific lineage.

We are reaching out to you not only as a pioneer but as an advisor. We would be honored to discuss how the Institute might align with your vision for democratization of genealogy. Whether through a formal advisory role or a partnership on archival content, your guidance would ensure this organization stays rooted in the highest standards of historical accuracy.

Success for us means that the discovery journeys you’ve popularized become accessible to every household. We’d love to share our current roadmap and MVP with you.

With deep respect,

[Lead's Name]
Executive Director, Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

## 2. LeVar Burton
**Angle:** Literacy, Legacy, and "Roots" Advocacy
**Subject:** Carrying the Legacy of "Roots" Forward

Dear Mr. Burton,

For generations, you have been the voice that connected us to our stories—from the indelible impact of *Roots* to the joy of reading. The Black Lineage & Heritage Institute was born from that same spirit of connection and the belief that knowing our story is a fundamental right.

We are building a toolset and archive specifically designed to help African Americans overcome the historical silences in the record. We are asking you to consider joining us as a Founding Ambassador. Your voice would help us reach families who believe their history is lost, reminding them that their stories are waiting to be found and told.

A social shout-out or a brief message of support from you would mean the world to our community. We are building the "Reading Rainbow" for our ancestors, and we’d love for you to be part of the journey.

Sincerely,

[Lead's Name]
Executive Director, Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

## 3. Ava DuVernay / Array
**Angle:** Historical Narrative & Storytelling
**Subject:** Reclaiming the Narrative: The Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

Dear Ms. DuVernay,

Through *13th*, *When They See Us*, and *Origin*, you have masterfully interrogated history to reclaim the present. At the Black Lineage & Heritage Institute, we are doing the same at the family level—helping individuals turn the "erasure" of the past into the "power" of rediscovered identity.

We are seeking a partnership with ARRAY or your personal support as a creative advisor. Our goal is to ensure that the data we collect isn't just stored in a database, but is used to fuel a new era of Black storytelling. We want to help families find the "scripts" of their own ancestors' lives.

Could we have 15 minutes to show you how our documentation tool is helping families build their own historical archives?

In solidarity,

[Lead's Name]
Executive Director, Black Lineage & Heritage Institute
