# Social Media Strategy: Black Lineage & Heritage Institute

## Objective
To build a vibrant, respectful, and engaged community around Black family history, establish the Institute as a thought leader in genealogy and heritage preservation, and drive interest in our tools and membership.

## Platform Strategy

### 1. Instagram (Visual Storytelling & Community)
- **Focus:** High-impact photography, archival documents, "then vs. now" family photos, and short video testimonials.
- **Content:** Reels showing how to use the genealogy tool, carousel posts on "Heritage Heroes," and beautiful quotes on identity.
- **Engagement:** Polls on Instagram Stories (e.g., "Do you know your great-grandparents' names?"), Q&A sessions with historians.

### 2. Twitter/X (News, Advocacy & Networking)
- **Focus:** Real-time updates, historical anniversaries, networking with genealogists, and participating in broader conversations about Black history.
- **Content:** Threads on specific historical records, live-tweeting genealogy events, and advocacy for preservation of Black burial grounds.
- **Engagement:** Using hashtags, tagging partners, and participating in #GenChat and #BlackHistory.

### 3. Facebook (Community Groups & Older Demographics)
- **Focus:** Building a supportive community for serious researchers and families.
- **Content:** Long-form stories, links to blog posts, and information about virtual workshops.
- **Engagement:** Creating a "Heritage Keepers" private group for members to share research tips and support each other.

### 4. LinkedIn (Professional Networking & Partnerships)
- **Focus:** B2B outreach, grant announcements, and professional milestones.
- **Content:** Impact reports, partnership announcements, and thought leadership on the importance of heritage in corporate DEI.
- **Engagement:** Connecting with academic institutions, other nonprofits, and potential corporate sponsors.

### 5. TikTok (Educational & Youth Outreach)
- **Focus:** Fast-paced, educational content to engage younger generations in their history.
- **Content:** "History bites" (60-second facts), "How-to" genealogy tips for beginners, and trends related to family legacy.
- **Engagement:** Stitching/Dueting with users sharing their family discoveries.

## Hashtag Strategy
Primary: `#BlackLineage` `#ReclaimOurRoots` `#HeritageKeepers`
Secondary: `#BlackGenealogy` `#AncestryStories` `#OurLegacy` `#FamilyHistory` `#PreserveThePast`

## Brand Voice
- **Dignified:** Respectful of the ancestors and the weight of history.
- **Empowering:** Focused on reclamation and the power of knowing one's story.
- **Accessible:** Removing the intimidation factor from genealogy research.
- **Communal:** Always emphasizing that we are doing this work together.
