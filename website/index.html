Turn the pain of erased histories into the power of rediscovered identity.

The Black Lineage & Heritage Institute exists to restore what was taken — names, stories, roots, and the unbroken chain of Black family history.

For generations, Black families were told their history began in chains. We know better.

The 1870 "Census Wall" stands as a barrier for many, but it is not the end of the story. Millions of records remain untapped, and thousands of stories are waiting to be reclaimed.

We are not defined by what was stolen from us. We are defined by what we rebuild.
